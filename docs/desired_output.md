## 🔴 Priority 0: MUST-HAVE (Mandatory Submission Fields)

*Every team must complete and upload these fields in `sinceai.app` before Sunday 10:00 EET to be eligible for judging and voting.*

### Form Fields & Text

**Project Name (Max 80 characters):** ZeroFault

**Tagline (Max 140 characters):** Smart retrofit platform transforming legacy industrial machines into self-diagnosing, zero-downtime assets.

**About the Project (Max 5,000 characters):** Structured description covering:

- The problem being addressed.
- Your solution and how it functions.
- How you built it (architecture, pipelines, engineering decisions).
- What hurdles or challenges you met on the way.
- What comes next / future roadmap.  
  *(Judges read all of it; voters see the first lines with a "Read more" toggle).*
  
**Built With:** Comma-separated list of technologies, frameworks, and libraries used. *(Note: The first 5 appear as badge chips on your public vote card).*

**Repository Link:** <https://github.com/romashkoyp/since_ai_2026>

**MLH Questions & GenAI Disclosure:**

- Complete the mandatory GenAI tool disclosure (list tools used, or select *"I did not use GenAI"*).
- State whether you implemented a generative AI model or API.
- Provide technology feedback where requested.

### Mandatory Files & Uploads

**Demo Video (YouTube link ONLY):**

- Must be a public or unlisted **YouTube URL**.
- **No file uploads** (MP4, WebM, MOV are not accepted).
- Ideally **under 2 minutes** in length.
**Source Code Archive (`.zip` file, Max 100 MB):**
- Exactly **one `.zip` file** containing the source code as it stands at the deadline.

## 🟡 Priority 1: HIGH PRIORITY (Voter & Judge Deciding Factors)

*These define "What makes a strong entry" according to the official Builder Guide evaluation principles.*

**1. Functional Working Prototype:**

- *"The product is the pitch."* Evaluation prioritizes a functioning, demonstratable software application over concept slides or wireframes.
**2. 20-Second Video Hook:**
- The demo video must show the **working product in the first 20 seconds**, and remain under two minutes in total length.
**3. 5-Second Tagline Clarity:**
- The 140-character tagline must be immediately understandable by peer voters in under five seconds.
**4. Repository README & Setup Instructions:**
- Clear, reproducible setup and run instructions allowing evaluators to run or inspect your code.
**5. Technology & AI Inventory in README:**
- Concise disclosure of:
  - Main AI development tools, models, and APIs used.
  - Frameworks, libraries, and their licenses.
  - Third-party datasets and external services.
  - Distinction between pre-existing code/assets and what was built during the hackathon.

## 🟢 Priority 2: OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED (Supplementary Material)

*Optional submission form uploads visible to voters or judges.*

**1. Pitch Deck (Optional PDF / PPT / PPTX, Max 1 file):**

- Uploaded directly in the submission form.
- Voters can open it directly from your vote card; judges review it alongside the submission.
- *(Rule: A deck is supplementary and never replaces a working prototype).*

**2. Live Demo URL (Optional):**

- Accessible web link where judges and evaluators can test the deployed application live.  
  *(Visible to judges; hidden from peer voters).*
  
**3. Supporting Documents (Optional, Up to 2 files):**

- Accepted formats: PDF, PPT, PPTX, DOC, DOCX, TXT, Markdown.
- Supplementary technical notes, benchmark reports, or architecture documentation for judges.

*Optional prize track categories and mandatory Sunday closing activities.*

**2. MLH Prize Categories (Optional Ticks):**
- Tick applicable MLH prize categories (zero, one, or several).
- Provide domain name, school affiliation, or Gemini API project number only if entering specific sponsor prize tracks.
**3. Pre-Deadline Review (Before Sun 10:00 EET):**
- Review all form fields, play the YouTube video, verify the ZIP archive.  
  *(Submissions can be edited freely in `sinceai.app` until the 10:00 EET hard deadline).*
**4. Peer Voting Participation (Sun 10:00 – 12:00 EET):**
- Every eligible builder must complete **20 pairwise project comparisons** in `sinceai.app`.
- The five highest-ranked projects from five different company challenges advance to Sunday's Finals (14:00 at EduCity).
